About VALORANT

What is VALORANT?

VALORANT is a free-to-play 5v5 character-based tactical hero shooter developed and published by Riot Games. Released in June 2020, VALORANT combines precise gunplay with unique character abilities to create a dynamic and strategic gaming experience. The game is designed for both casual players and competitive esports enthusiasts.

Core Gameplay Elements

At its heart, VALORANT is a tactical shooter where two teams of five players compete to either plant a bomb-like entity called the "Spike" and defend it until detonation, or prevent the opposing team from doing so. Each round lasts 100 seconds, and teams win by either eliminating all opponents or achieving their objective.

  • Tactical Gameplay: Strategy and positioning matter as much as aim and reflexes
  • Ability-Based Combat: Each agent has unique abilities that define their role and playstyle
  • Economy System: Players earn credits each round to purchase weapons and abilities strategically
  • Team Coordination: Communication and teamwork are essential for victory

About Riot Games

Riot Games is a Los Angeles-based video game publisher and developer. Founded in 2006, Riot Games is best known for creating League of Legends, one of the world's most popular esports titles. The company focuses on creating competitive games with strong esports potential and engaging community experiences.

With VALORANT, Riot Games brought their expertise in competitive gaming to the tactical shooter genre, creating a game that emphasizes skill, strategy, and fair competition.

History & Impact

Launch: VALORANT was released on June 2, 2020, following a closed beta period that generated massive community interest. The game quickly became a sensation in the gaming world, attracting millions of players and establishing itself as a major esports title.

Global Expansion: Available in multiple languages and regions worldwide, VALORANT has become a global phenomenon. The game's esports scene includes regional competitions and international tournaments with millions of dollars in prize pools.

Continuous Development: Riot Games regularly updates VALORANT with new agents, maps, game modes, and balance changes to keep the game fresh and competitive.

Why Play VALORANT?

For Competitive Players

Ranked competitive mode with skill-based ranking system, esports tournaments with massive prize pools, and a thriving professional scene.

For Casual Players

Unrated matches, Spike Rush mode for quick games, Deathmatch for practice, and a fun community to play with friends.

Free-to-Play Model

No paywall to play. Optional cosmetics and battle pass system support development without affecting gameplay.

Anti-Cheat System

State-of-the-art anti-cheat technology ensures fair play and maintains competitive integrity across all matches.

Community & Esports

VALORANT has a vibrant and passionate community. The game's esports scene includes:

  • International Tournaments: VCT (VALORANT Champions Tour) - the official global esports ecosystem
  • Regional Competitions: Localized tournaments in multiple regions around the world
  • Streamer Community: Popular content creators and streamers regularly broadcast VALORANT gameplay
  • Community Events: Fan-created tournaments, community challenges, and special events
