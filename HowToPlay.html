How to Play VALORANT

Game Objective

VALORANT is a 5v5 tactical shooter where two teams, the Attackers and the Defenders, battle for control of the map. The Attacking team's primary objective is to plant a bomb-like device called the "Spike" at one of two designated bomb sites (A or B). The Defending team must prevent the Spike from being planted or defuse it if planted.

A team wins a round by either:

  • Eliminating all members of the opposing team
  • Successfully planting the Spike (Attackers) and it detonates
  • Defusing the Spike before it detonates (Defenders)
  • Timer expiring with the Spike not planted (Defenders win)
  • Spike timer expiring before detonation (Defenders win)

Understanding Game Rounds

VALORANT matches consist of 25 rounds (sometimes 13 rounds in certain modes). Teams play 12-13 rounds as attackers and 12-13 rounds as defenders. The first team to win 13 rounds wins the match.

Each Round Duration: 100 seconds to complete the round objective or eliminate opponents.

Buy Phase: At the start of each round, players have time to purchase weapons, armor, and abilities using credits earned from previous rounds.

Basic Controls & Movement

Movement

  • W: Move Forward
  • A: Move Left
  • S: Move Backward
  • D: Move Right
  • SPACE: Jump
  • CTRL: Crouch
  • SHIFT: Walk Silently

Combat

  • Mouse 1: Fire Weapon
  • Mouse 2: Aim Down Sights
  • R: Reload
  • E: Use / Interact
  • Q/C/X/F: Use Abilities
  • TAB: See Scoreboard

The Economy System

VALORANT features an economy system where players earn credits each round based on performance. Credits are used to purchase weapons, armor, and abilities. Understanding economy management is crucial for success.

Action Credits Earned
Planting Spike 300 credits
Defusing Spike 100 credits
Eliminating Opponent (Kill) 250-300 credits
First Blood (First Kill) 350 credits
Winning Round 3,200 credits

Note: Credits carry over between rounds up to a maximum of 9,000 credits.

Weapon Types

VALORANT features four main weapon categories. Each has different price points and purposes.

Sidearms

Pistols used as starting weapons. Classic, Shorty, Frenzy, Ghost

Rifles

Primary weapons with balanced damage and accuracy. Vandal, Phantom

SMGs

Close-range weapons with high fire rate. Stinger, Spectre

Sniper Rifles

Long-range high-damage weapons. Operator, Marshal

Agents & Their Abilities

In VALORANT, you select an Agent at the start of each match. Each agent has four unique abilities:

  • Two Ability Slots (Q & C): Purchasable abilities that players can buy each round
  • One Signature Ability (X): A powerful ability that regenerates or can be purchased
  • Ultimate Ability (F): An extremely powerful ability that charges by collecting orbs and dealing/taking damage

There are four Agent roles: Duelists (entry fraggers), Initiators (information gatherers), Controllers (map control), and Sentinels (defensive support). Choose agents that complement your playstyle and team composition.

Beginner Tips & Tactics

  1. Communicate with Your Team: Use voice chat or messages to coordinate with teammates
  2. Play as a Team: Don't charge in alone; stick with teammates for mutual support
  3. Learn the Maps: Spend time learning map layouts, callouts, and positioning
  4. Practice Aiming: Spend time in the shooting range to improve your aim and recoil control
  5. Manage Your Economy: Don't waste credits; coordinate team purchases for optimal economy management
  6. Use Abilities Wisely: Don't waste powerful abilities; use them strategically with your team
  7. Play Your Role: Stick to your agent's role to maximize team synergy
  8. Practice & Patience: VALORANT has a learning curve; practice consistently to improve

Different Game Modes

VALORANT offers multiple game modes suited to different preferences:

  • Unrated: Casual matches without rank impact. Perfect for learning and practice
  • Competitive: Ranked matches where your skill rating changes based on performance
  • Spike Rush: Fast-paced 6-round matches with random loadouts and shared money
  • Deathmatch: Free-for-all mode where players respawn after elimination
  • Team Deathmatch: Team-based FFA mode focused on combat skills
