VALORANT

A 5v5 tactical hero shooter by Riot Games

Welcome to VALORANT

VALORANT is a free-to-play 5v5 character-based tactical shooter. Outwit, outplay, and outgun your opponents across multiple game modes. Choose your agent, master your abilities, and lead your team to victory.

Key Features

  • Strategic Gameplay with tactical abilities
  • Diverse Agent Selection with unique abilities
  • Dynamic Map Design with interactive elements
  • Competitive and Casual Game Modes
  • Free-to-Play with Battle Pass System
  • Cross-Platform Play and Support

Game Modes Overview

Game Mode Description Team Size Duration
Unrated Casual match with no rank impact 5v5 ~30 minutes
Competitive Ranked match with rank progression 5v5 ~30-50 minutes
Spike Rush Fast-paced mode with random loadouts 5v5 ~8-12 minutes
Deathmatch Free-for-all combat mode 6-14 Players ~10 minutes
Team Deathmatch Team-based FFA mode 5v5 ~10 minutes
