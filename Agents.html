Choosing Your Agent

  • Start with agents that have simple, straightforward abilities
  • Practice with different roles to find what suits your playstyle
  • Consider team composition - don't duplicate roles unless necessary
  • Master a few agents before trying to learn them all
  • Watch pro players to learn advanced agent strategies
